# Rock Fever crawler policy
# - Search engines, and AI tools that look pages up to answer someone's question,
#   may read the public pages so people can find the next event.
# - AI model training is not allowed: the pages show photos and names of members.
# - /admin and /login are for the team only.
# Content-Signal (https://contentsignals.org) is advisory: it is only followed by crawlers that support it.

User-agent: *
Content-Signal: search=yes, ai-input=yes, ai-train=no
Allow: /
Disallow: /admin
Disallow: /login
